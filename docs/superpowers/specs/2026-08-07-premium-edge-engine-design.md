# Sooth Premium Edge Engine — Design

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Approved (design); ready for implementation planning
**Context:** Upscaling the abandoned "LockCity Picks" paid-picks Discord into a real
business built on Sooth's honest, provable foundation. LockCity had no following and
no real edge (its model's CLV was negative). Sooth already avoids picks-selling's
legal and credibility traps by design; this spec adds a curated premium layer without
breaking that.

---

## Goal

One betting brand — Sooth — with:
- a **free, teaching-first board** anyone can learn to read (widest top-of-funnel), and
- a **premium tier** delivering sealed "best plays," graded honestly.

The premium promise is **not a win-rate claim**. It is **"% of our flagged plays that
beat the closing line"** — provable, legal (no FTC performance-claim burden), and
genuinely strong when the engine is good. Selling *tools + curated data*, never picks
presented as profitable.

## Non-goals

- No prediction/forecasting model to generate plays. Sooth already measured that its
  model loses to the close (49.5% ATS); rebuilding on it re-introduces the exact risk
  we avoid.
- No win/loss "record flex." No "locks," "guaranteed," "risk-free."
- Not a sportsbook. Never accept, place, or facilitate a wager. Never hold funds.

---

## 1. Architecture — one new module

Everything sits on Sooth's existing `engine/`. The only substantial new brain is
**`engine/edges.py`**. Sooth stays static files + append-only data; no new backend.

Daily flow:

```
capture board (existing capture.py / lines.py)
      → edges.py ranks every market by edge
            ├─ FREE: full ranked board published to the site (the teaching map)
            └─ PREMIUM: top N plays selected → sealed via commit.py (existing Merkle)
                        → after games settle: grade.py grades on CLV (existing)
                        → "% beat the close" regenerated by published_figures.py
```

`edges.py` is the wedge. Capture, closing-line tracking, Merkle sealing, grading, and
calibration already exist and are reused.

## 2. The two edges + the honest number

Per market, `edges.py` computes:

- **Price gap** — best available book price vs the de-vigged fair (consensus) line.
  Is this side underpriced relative to the market's own fair estimate?
- **Shop gap** — spread between the best and worst book on the same side. Pure
  line-shopping value; +EV on arithmetic alone, no forecast required.

"Best plays" = top-ranked by price gap (the curated premium selection). Graded **only**
on beat-the-close %, never win/loss. The published figure is regenerated by
`scripts/published_figures.py` so it can never be hand-edited (Sooth hard-limit #2).

The exact ranking formula (weighting, thresholds, top-N count) is deferred to the
implementation plan.

## 3. Free vs Premium

**Free (the funnel + teaching):**
- The full board, with the two edges shown **as columns on the board itself** — no
  separate "edge tab." The edge lives next to the price; reading the board *is* the
  lesson.
- "Learn to read it" explainers.
- Biggest line-shopping gaps of the day.

**Premium (the upgrade):**
- The daily **sealed top plays** (game lines at launch).
- Line-movement alerts.
- The running **beat-the-close scoreboard** (the honest number).
- Player-prop plays as they go live (see §4).

## 4. Player props — staged, quality-gated

Built in parallel with game lines (per user decision), but with a hard gate: **prop
plays only become premium-live once they clear a verified beat-the-close bar on real
closing data.** Until then they are internal/backtest only. Game lines never wait on
props. This follows Sooth's charter (never promote a market to "Live" without free,
verifiable closing-line history to grade it).

Props carry higher data cost (more Odds API credits, more markets, faster movement).
Credit discipline from the charter still binds: ≤400 credits/run, ≥3,000 in reserve.

## 5. The new community

Delete the old LockCity Discord; spin up a fresh one **branded to Sooth**. Structure
serves the funnel, reusing LockCity's proven webhook + scheduler plumbing:

- **Public "learn the edge" channel** — board reads, teaching (the magnet).
- **Public "line-move alerts" channel** — auto-posted from the engine.
- **Premium-only "sealed plays" channel** — gated by the subscription.

No picks-flexing anywhere.

## 6. Premium delivery + payments

Subscription on sooth.bet. The **tools-not-picks framing is load-bearing for payment
processing too**: Sooth is a data/tools publisher, not a gambling operator, which is
what keeps processors comfortable. Reuse Whop (already set up, KYC complete) or Stripe
under the tools-not-picks framing. Confirm processor terms before launch — betting-
*adjacent* is fine; betting-*operator* is not, and Sooth is the former.

Delivery of sealed plays to premium: gated Discord channel and/or a gated page on the
site. Exact mechanism deferred to the plan.

## 7. Scope / timeline

**Launch floor — by NFL Week 1 (2026-09-09):**
- Game-line edges + line-shopping live on the site (board with edge columns).
- Premium sealed plays for game lines.
- New Sooth Discord.
- Beat-the-close scoreboard.

**Fast-follow:**
- Player props flip to premium-live the day they clear the beat-close bar.
