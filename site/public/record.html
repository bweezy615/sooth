Our model does not beat the market.

We run a prediction model and we grade it in public against the market's closing price with the books' built-in cut stripped out (called "de-vigging": removing the house margin to find the true fair odds). ATS means "against the spread" — did the pick cover the point spread? On both samples below, no version wins often enough to beat the 52.38% ATS you need just to break even at standard -110 odds, and the market's stated chances match real results more closely than ours do. That is the whole point: what you pay for is the analysis and the board, not a forecast we can't honestly deliver. Every figure here is regenerated by one command and lives in a file you can download.
