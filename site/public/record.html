The record

Our model does not beat the market.

We run a prediction model and we grade it in public against the market's closing price with the books' built-in cut stripped out (the "de-vigged" line). On both samples below, no version wins often enough to beat the 52.38% you need just to break even, and the market's stated chances match real results more closely than ours do. That is the whole point: what you pay for is the board (the best price across books), not a forecast we can't honestly promise. Every figure here is regenerated by one command and lives in a file you can download.

Expected calibration error is the gap between what we predict and what actually happens. Lower is better. The de-vigged market beats our best model on both samples. We publish this rather than the one framing where we'd look good, because a number nobody can check is a claim, not a measurement.
