HOW IT WORKS · 60-SECOND GUIDE · NO JARGON

Same bet, different prices. We show you the best one.

Every sportsbook prices the same game a little differently. Sooth reads them all, shows you the best price on each side, and analyzes what the numbers already say. You don't need any math to use it.

01 · THE ONE IDEA

Every bet has a "fair price." The fair price is what the odds should be if the sportsbook took no cut. When a book offers you a better number than fair, you're getting a deal — you win a little more when it hits, and risk a little less. Sooth finds the book with the best number so you're not leaving money on the table.

02 · THE BOOK'S CUT

The book always keeps a little. We show you how much. Two even sides at -110 each look like a coin flip, but they're not: -110 is really about 52.4% to hit, and 52.4% + 52.4% = 104.8%. That extra ~4.8% is the book's cut. We strip it out to show the true, fair price, then find the book beating it by the most.

-110 & -110  →  52.4% + 52.4% = 104.8%
the book's cut = ~4.8%  ·  we remove it to find the fair price

03 · WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING FOR

"Gap points" — the size of the deal. For each side, the market shows the points of implied probability you gain by taking the best price instead of the worst one on the board. Bigger is a better deal. The green number in the BEST column is the price, and the small tag next to it names the book offering it.

04 · READING THE MARKET

Green is the price to take. Each row on the market is one game: the FAIR column is the price with the book's cut removed, the BEST column is the strongest number posted and which book has it, and GAP PTS is how far the books disagree. Tap any game for the full analysis — form, availability, every book's price on one scale, and how the number has moved. Or just ask the analyzer on the front page, in plain English.
