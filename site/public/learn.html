How it works

Same bet, different prices. We show you the best one.

Every sportsbook prices the same game a little differently. Sooth reads them all and shows you the best price on each side, plus how good that price really is. That's the whole idea. You don't need any math to use it.

THE ONE IDEA

Every bet has a "fair price."

The fair price is what the odds should be if the sportsbook took no cut. When a book offers you a better number than fair, you're getting a deal — you win a little more when it hits, and risk a little less. Sooth finds the book with the best number so you're not leaving money on the table.

THE BOOK'S CUT

The book always keeps a little. We show you how much.

Two even sides at -110 each look like a coin flip, but they're not: -110 is really about 52.4% to hit, and 52.4% + 52.4% = 104.8%. That extra ~4.8% is the book's cut. We strip it out to show the true, fair price, then find the book beating it by the most.

WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING FOR

"Points you gain" — the size of the deal.

For each pick we show the points you gain by taking the best price instead of the fair one. Bigger is a better deal. To keep it simple, every pick also gets a plain label:

Great value a clearly better-than-fair price
Slight value a small edge over fair
READING THE BOARD

Green is the price to take.

On the board, the sport ball (⚾ 🏈 🏒 🥊) tells you the sport at a glance, the green number is the best available price and where to get it, and the label tells you how good the deal is. Start in Beginner view for just the essentials, or switch to Pro for every column.

One important thing: sooth.bet is tools and data, not picks. A better-than-fair price is good value, but no bet is ever certain and nothing here predicts a winner. We also grade our own model in the open, and it does not beat the closing market (the final odds before a game starts). That is the honest part, and it is why we provide the tools, not a forecast.
