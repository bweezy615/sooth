We publish our own model losing. Across 2,671 graded walk-forward NFL games (2,608 decided, 63 pushes) our prediction model hit 49.5% of decided games against the spread; the market hit 50.9%; breakeven is 52.4%. Nobody clears it. That measurement is why Sooth sells price transparency instead of picks — and why you can trust the rest of this site: numbers that flatter us and numbers that do not get published the same way.
