Where the books disagree with each other.

An arb is two books whose best prices on opposite sides add to less than 100% — betting both captures the gap. A middle is two books whose lines leave a gap — land inside it and both bets win. Both are observations about disagreement, not predictions.

Live arbitrage — moneylines
