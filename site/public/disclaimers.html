Disclaimers and disclosures

Nine disclosures. They are written to be read rather than to be skipped, and they are linked from every page of this site rather than buried in a terms document. If any statement here ever conflicts with marketing copy elsewhere on this site, this page governs and the marketing copy is the error.

1. Entertainment and analysis only

This site publishes statistical predictions, calibrated probabilities, and analytical tools for informational and entertainment purposes. Nothing here is betting advice, financial advice, investment advice, or a recommendation to place any wager or enter any transaction.

We do not tell you what to bet. We publish what our model estimates the probability of an outcome to be, alongside what the market estimates, and we publish the measured accuracy of both. What you do with that information is entirely your decision and entirely your responsibility.

2. We do not accept wagers, hold funds, or pay prizes

We are a publisher, not a gambling operator.

We do not accept, place, broker, or facilitate bets. We do not hold customer funds, operate a wallet, run a contest with an entry fee, or pay out winnings, prizes, or anything of value based on the outcome of a sporting event. We hold no gaming licence in any jurisdiction because we conduct no activity that requires one.

The only money that changes hands between you and us is a subscription fee for access to software tools and data, described in section 9. That fee is not a stake, is not pooled, and does not vary with any sporting result.

3. Past performance does not indicate future results

Every historical figure on this site - backtest results, calibration statistics, graded records - describes what has already happened. None of it predicts what will happen next.

Our published backtest covers 2,750 out-of-sample games from 2016 through 2025. In that sample, our model did not beat the closing betting market. It recorded a Brier score of 0.22228 against the de-vigged market's 0.21061, and an against-the-spread record of 1333-1352-65, or 49.65%, against a 52.38% breakeven at standard -110 pricing. The full figures and the methods behind them are on the methodology page.

We make no claim, express or implied, that using this site will make you money, reduce your losses, or improve your results. We make no win-rate claim, no return-on-investment claim, and no profitability claim of any kind. Sports betting is a losing proposition for the overwhelming majority of participants over time. Nothing on this site changes that.

Words that will never appear in our copy as descriptions of our output: guaranteed, lock, risk-free, insider, sure thing.

4. Age and jurisdiction

This site is intended for adults aged 21 or over.

Sports wagering is legal in some jurisdictions and illegal in others, and the legal minimum age differs between them. It is your responsibility to know and follow the laws that apply where you are. We do not verify your location, we do not verify your age beyond your own affirmation, and we do not encourage anyone to place a wager anywhere it is unlawful for them to do so.

Access from a jurisdiction where sports wagering is prohibited does not make reading published statistics unlawful, but it does not make wagering lawful either. When in doubt, consult the rules that apply to you.

6. Responsible gambling

Gambling can be addictive. If it has stopped being entertainment for you, or if you are betting money you cannot afford to lose, help is free and confidential.

1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) - the helpline operated by the Council on Compulsive Gambling of New Jersey (CCGNJ), available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

1-800-522-4700 - the National Problem Gambling Helpline, operated by the National Council on Problem Gambling (NCPG). Call or text, 24 hours a day. Chat is available at ncpgambling.org/chat.

If you are in immediate crisis, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the United States.

Both national helplines route to local resources and are free. Most jurisdictions with legal sports wagering also operate a self-exclusion programme that blocks your access to licensed operators for a period you choose; the helplines above can explain how to enrol.

We will never present gambling as a way to make money, solve a financial problem, or recover a loss. Chasing losses is the most reliable way to make them worse.

7. How our record is produced and what it proves

Every prediction we publish is hashed and committed to a public SHA-256 Merkle root before the first kickoff of its slate, and that root is anchored to a third-party timestamp in a public Git repository. After the games settle we publish the predictions in full. Anyone can recompute the root from the published predictions and confirm it matches what we committed to in advance. Instructions are on the verification page.

This means our record is complete and tamper-evident. We cannot delete a losing prediction, add a winning one after the fact, or quietly change a probability.

It does not mean our predictions are accurate. Cryptographic commitment makes our reporting honest; it says nothing about the quality of the underlying model, and our own testing says that model does not beat the market.

Additional limits on what our record shows:

  • Predictions are graded against the sources named on the methodology page.
  • We do not currently publish a closing-line-value figure. Our historical line data comes from a source that overwrites line values in place as the market moves, which makes any CLV number computed from it unreliable. We will publish CLV when we can validate it against an independent source with explicit opening and closing records, and not before.
  • Only NFL is graded against verified closing lines. Every other sport on this site carries a status label of "in calibration" or "deferred" and its predictions are explicitly unproven.
  • Corrections to the graded record are published as corrections, with the original figure shown alongside.

8. Material connections and endorsements (16 CFR Part 255)

Under the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, specifically 16 CFR 255.5 on disclosure of material connections, we disclose the following.

Affiliate and referral relationships. Where we receive compensation for a referral, click, or sign-up from any third party, including any sportsbook, odds provider, data vendor, or software product, that relationship is disclosed clearly and conspicuously at the point of the link, not only on this page. If a link is not marked as compensated, we are not being paid for it.

No paid placement in predictions or rankings. No third party can pay to influence a published probability, a graded result, a status label, or a position in any comparison or ranking on this site. Our predictions are generated by a documented model from public data, and the model does not know who our commercial partners are.

Testimonials and reviews. We do not publish testimonials in exchange for compensation, free access, or any other consideration. Where a user statement appears on this site, it is unedited except for length, published with that person's permission, and accompanied by a disclosure of any relationship they have with us. We do not use stock imagery or invented personas to represent customers. We do not publish screenshots of winnings, ours or anyone else's, as evidence of anything.

Our own commercial interest. We sell subscriptions to analytical tools. That is a material connection between us and everything we publish, and you should read this site with that in mind - which is precisely why the underlying prediction record is cryptographically verifiable rather than self-reported.

Artificial intelligence claims. We describe our system accurately. It is an Elo rating model with isotonic probability calibration, and we call it that. We do not describe it as artificial intelligence, machine learning, or a neural network, because it is none of those, and we do not attribute capabilities to it that we have not measured and published.

9. Subscriptions, automatic renewal, and cancellation

Paid subscriptions on this site provide access to analytical tools and data. They do not provide picks that we claim will win, because we make no such claim about any prediction, free or paid.

Before you are charged, we disclose clearly and conspicuously: the full price, the billing frequency, that the subscription renews automatically until cancelled, the date of the first renewal charge, and how to cancel. You must give separate affirmative consent to the automatic-renewal terms by ticking a box specific to those terms. Consent to renewal is never bundled into acceptance of general terms of service, and the box is never pre-ticked.

One-click cancellation, same medium. You can cancel online, at any time, in the same place you subscribed, in one step, without speaking to anyone, without a retention call, without an email request, and without navigating a sequence of offers. The cancel control is in your account settings. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access until then.

Renewal reminders. For subscription terms of one year or longer, and before any material change to price or terms, we send an advance reminder by email stating what is about to be charged, when, and how to cancel.

Refunds. If you are charged after cancelling, or charged an amount that does not match what was disclosed to you, contact us and we will refund it. We do not require you to dispute a charge with your bank to get an error corrected.

These practices are our implementation of the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act (ROSCA) and California's automatic-renewal requirements under AB 2863. Where the law of your jurisdiction gives you stronger rights than the paragraphs above, those rights apply.
