New to this?

The fair price is what the odds would be if the books took no cut. We work it out by comparing every book pricing the same line and stripping the margin out.

Below fair is normal. That gap is the house's cut — it is on almost every price you will ever see. The number worth having is the smallest one.

Taking the best price is worth money on its own, whether or not the bet wins. Two books offering the same bet at different numbers pay differently, and that difference is the only thing on this page that does not depend on predicting anything. The 60-second guide covers the rest.
