Independent is our own model — Elo plus opponent-adjusted efficiency. It has never seen the betting line, and it is measurably worse than the market. Consensus adds the de-vigged market price: better calibrated, but it largely reproduces the market. Publishing only the consensus would be market-following dressed up as insight, so we publish both. Where they disagree, treat it as a map of our blind spots rather than a tip.
