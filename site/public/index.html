Method

Two numbers. Both checkable.

Best price

The most generous quote on a side across every book we read. Same bet, same outcome — one book simply pays more.

Taking it costs nothing and requires no opinion about who wins.

—
Average gain this board

Fair line

Every posted price carries the book's margin, so it always understates your real chances. We take the median across books and remove the margin.

Median, not average — one stale quote near kickoff shouldn't move a consensus.
